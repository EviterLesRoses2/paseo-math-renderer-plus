Math Renderer Plus displays inline and block LaTeX in completed Paseo replies, including Chinese labels inside formulas. Formula rendering has been verified on a real iPhone in the native Paseo iOS app. The Paseo host generates PNGs locally and sends them to the app for display.

Requires Paseo 0.13 or newer with plugins enabled on the selected host. Git installation also needs Node.js 22 or later and npm on that host.

Block formulas are centered. Hover over a formula on desktop or tap it on iPhone to reveal the actions for viewing and copying LaTeX. Wide formulas and tables scroll horizontally. Unsupported commands, missing glyphs, and size limits preserve the original source. Enable one assistant-message formula renderer per host.
